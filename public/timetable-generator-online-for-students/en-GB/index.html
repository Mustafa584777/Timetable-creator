Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is this Student Timetable Generator completely free to use?

Yes, 100% free with unlimited timetable creation, custom color styling, and high-resolution PDF/PNG exports. No registration, login, or payment details are required.

Can I use this for university lectures with irregular class timings?

Yes! The generator supports flexible 15-minute, 30-minute, and 60-minute time intervals, enabling you to accurately map irregular college lectures, rotating bi-weekly labs, and evening tutorial sessions.

How do I print my student timetable or export it as a PDF?

Simply click the "Export" button in the top action toolbar and choose "Export to PDF" or "Export to PNG". The file renders in an optimized, print-ready format that looks sharp on standard A4 or Letter paper.

Will my student schedule remain saved if I refresh or close my browser?

Yes. All schedule edits are continuously auto-saved locally in your browser storage (LocalStorage). When you return, your full timetable reloads instantly. You can also download a JSON backup file anytime.

Can I include weekend days for coaching classes and exam revision?

Yes. In the days settings panel, you can switch between a Monday–Friday school week and a 7-day full week to schedule Saturday coaching, library study sessions, and Sunday exam revisions.
