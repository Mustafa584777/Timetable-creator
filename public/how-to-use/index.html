5 Steps to Create Your Perfect Schedule

Everything you need to transform a chaotic week into an organized, color-coded timetable.

1
Step 1

Select Your Timetable Planner Mode

Timetable Creator provides two specialized scheduling engines. Choose the one that matches your current goal:

Free Online Timetable Maker

Best for general weekly routines, work shifts, home study habits, workout blocks, and daily task management with custom hourly intervals.

Student Timetable Generator

Designed specifically for schools, colleges, and universities with support for subject names, teacher names, room codes, and period-based slots.

2
Step 2

Configure Active Days & Time Slots

Before adding subjects, align your timetable grid with your institution's timing or your personal schedule:

  • Days of the Week: Toggle Monday through Friday (standard school week), Monday through Saturday (university schedule), or all 7 days for weekend study.
  • Start & End Hours: Set when your day begins (e.g., 08:00 AM) and when your final session concludes (e.g., 05:00 PM).
  • Interval Length: Pick 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 60 minutes, or 90 minutes per block to match your school period bell timings.
3
Step 3

Add Classes, Subjects & Activity Blocks

Populating your timetable is seamless and intuitive:

Click any slot: Click any cell on the grid to open the fast event editor dialog.

Fill in details: Enter your course title (e.g., Advanced Mathematics), instructor name (e.g., Prof. Anderson), and room code (e.g., Hall 204).

Color Coding: Assign distinct pastel or vibrant colors to each subject (e.g., Blue for Math, Emerald for Biology, Amber for History, Rose for Breaks). Color coding allows your eye to recognise schedule patterns in fractions of a second.

Repeat & Duplicate: Need the same class on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday? Use the quick duplicate button to replicate blocks without retyping.

4
Step 4

Supercharge with AI Smart Schedule Generation

Don't want to place every subject manually? Use our built-in natural language AI schedule generator:

"Create a balanced university timetable with Computer Science on Mon/Wed 10:00-11:30 in Lab 3, Physics on Tue/Thu 09:00-10:30, and a 1-hour lunch break every day at 12:00."

Type your prompt into the AI prompt bar, click Generate, and the AI algorithm will analyze conflicts, insert lunch intervals, and automatically distribute your courses evenly across the week.

5
Step 5

Save, Print & Export Your Timetable

Once your routine is finalized, export it in multiple versatile formats:

Download HD PNG

Crystal-clear image format, ideal for mobile phone lockscreens, tablet backgrounds, or messaging apps.

Print / Save as PDF

Formatted cleanly for A4 and Letter paper. Perfect for pinning on study desk pinboards or classroom walls.

Encrypted Share Link

Send an instant link to friends or classmates so they can view or clone your schedule into their own planner.

Scheduling Tips for Every User

Proven recommendations to help you get the most out of your timetable.

For School & College Students

  • • Add Study Buffers: Don't just schedule class lectures; allocate 45-minute independent revision blocks right after hard subjects.
  • • Save to Lock Screen: Download the timetable as PNG and set it as your smartphone wallpaper during exam week so you never miss a lecture hall.
  • • Color by Difficulty: Mark challenging subjects with bold red or amber colors to mentally prepare before entering class.

For Teachers & Faculty

  • • Include Classroom Codes: Always fill in room numbers and lab designations to avoid double-booking physical school spaces.
  • • Mark Office Hours: Block dedicated 60-minute student consultation periods so your week remains structured and stress-free.
  • • Export as PDF: Print the master schedule in landscape format to display outside departmental bulletin boards.

For Parents & Homeschoolers

  • • Balance Academics & Play: Alternate academic focus sessions with 20-minute movement, outdoor, or snack breaks.
  • • Visual Consistency: Use child-friendly colors (bright yellow, green, orange) to make following the routine fun and rewarding.
  • • Pin on the Fridge: Print a fresh copy every Sunday evening and review the upcoming week together with your children.

For Professionals & Freelancers

  • • Time Blocking: Dedicate deep work mornings (09:00 - 12:00) with zero meetings, and group client calls into the afternoons.
  • • Cloud Sync: Sign in with Google so your timetable syncs seamlessly across your desktop laptop and phone browser.
  • • Respect Weekends: Keep Saturday and Sunday distinct from Monday-Friday to prevent remote work burnout.

Do I need to install any software or app? ▾

No installation is required. Timetable Creator is 100% web-based and runs smoothly inside Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, and all mobile browsers without downloading any APK or executable.

Will my schedule be erased if I refresh or close my browser? ▾

No! The application automatically saves your active schedule to your browser's local cache. If you log in with your account, your schedules are saved permanently in our secure cloud Firestore database so you can access them from any computer or mobile device.

Is Timetable Creator completely free? ▾

Yes! All core scheduling features, including AI generation, color styling, PNG downloads, and PDF exports, are 100% free with no credit card required.
