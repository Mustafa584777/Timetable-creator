Online School Timetable Maker for Teachers & Principals

Creating a weekly school schedule by hand is one of the most tedious administrative challenges for academic coordinators. Between balancing core subjects, ensuring certified teachers aren't double-booked, distributing difficult lectures evenly, and scheduling daily recesses and lunch periods, manual spreadsheets inevitably create scheduling headaches.

Our School Timetable Maker for Teachers solves this with an intelligent constraint-solving algorithm. Simply pick your active days (Monday–Friday or Monday–Saturday), specify school start and end times, choose period slot lengths (such as 30, 45, or 60 minutes), and list your subjects along with optional teacher names. Click Generate Timetable to automatically produce a balanced, color-coded weekly roster where no subject is assigned twice in a row and breaks are scheduled with clockwork precision.

⚖️ Even Subject Distribution

Distributes math, science, language arts, and history proportionally across the school week so students stay engaged and avoid lecture fatigue.

🚫 No Back-to-Back Duplicates

Enforces strict single-period constraints so the same subject will never appear twice consecutively on the same day unless specifically combined.

☕ Automatic Recess & Lunch Breaks

Automatically inserts 15-minute recess breaks or lunch periods after every 2, 3, or 4 lecture slots to match your institution's bell schedule.

How to Build Your School Schedule in 4 Minutes

1. Set Active Days & Hours

Select Monday through Friday (or include Saturday) and configure your school's daily start time (e.g. 8:00 AM) and dismissal time (e.g. 3:00 PM).

2. Define Periods & Break Rules

Choose 45-minute bell periods and select automatic break intervals (such as a 15-minute recess after every 2 periods).

3. Enter Subjects & Faculty

Type your curriculum subjects (Mathematics, Science, English, History, Art, P.E.) and assign corresponding subject teachers.

4. Generate & Distribute

Click "Generate Timetable" to produce the conflict-free schedule. If you want a fresh permutation, simply tap "Regenerate".

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this School Timetable Maker free to use? +
Yes, this online school timetable builder is 100% free with no subscription, registration, or credit card required. You can generate unlimited timetables, customize subjects, and export clean CSVs and prints.
Can I customize the slot duration for bell schedules? +
Yes. You can choose period lengths of 30, 40, 45, 50, or 60 minutes. The builder automatically calculates start and end times for each period and break slot throughout the school day.
How does the auto-break feature work? +
When you set "Break After Every 2 slots", the timetable generator automatically inserts a standardized 15-minute break row across all days after every pair of teaching periods, keeping schedules uniform and easy to read.
Can I export the timetable to Excel or Google Sheets? +
Yes! Click the "Download CSV" button at any time to save a comma-separated values file that opens directly in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice, or Apple Numbers.
Can I print the timetable on A4 paper for classroom display? +
Yes. Simply click the "Print" button or press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac). The tool includes dedicated print styles that hide web controls, format cleanly in landscape, and optimize contrast for physical printers.
